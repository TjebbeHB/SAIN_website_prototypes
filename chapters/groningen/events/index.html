SAIN Groningen

Every event the chapter has run

Everything before 30 April 2026 was organised as the AI Safety Initiative Groningen (AISIG); everything after it continues as SAIN Groningen. Each entry keeps the chapter’s own announcement from the time, so the wording is the wording it went out with.

  • Monday, February 19, 2024, 5:00 PM 7:00 PM

    Regulating New Surveillance Technologies at Work

    From the announcement at the time

    In collaboration with Panoptiwork and STeP. Sandy Gould from the University of Cardiff will delve into the evolution of surveillance technologies, challenging the traditional critiques by highlighting the modern changes in workplace measurement. Following him, Michele Molè from the University of Groningen will address the legal implications within the EU, aiming to protect workers' privacy and rights.
